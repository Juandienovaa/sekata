# Brand Specification — Sekata Coffee & Slowbar (CTA Edition)

## 1. Brand Identity & Soul
- **Name**: Sekata Coffee & Slowbar
- **Location**: Tanjungpinang, Kepulauan Riau (0°55'18"N 104°27'06"E)
- **Tagline**: "Setiap Seduhan Punya Cerita" • "#SatuMejaBerhentiSejenak" • "#KecilBertumbuh"
- **Essence**: An intimate communal slowbar where conversation and craft coffee merge. Honest, tactile, warm, unpretentious, human.

## 2. Palette
- **Paper Cream**: `#fff9f2` (Primary background, warm print texture)
- **Deep Espresso**: `#522c25` (Primary text, high-contrast woodcut feel)
- **Signature Turquoise**: `#317576` (Brand accent, active states, buttons)
- **Sunlit Marigold**: `#f5ba31` (Extracted from `CTA.jpg` sweater, energetic warmth)
- **Linen Beige**: `#f6ede3` (Card backgrounds, subtle frames)
- **Hairline Taupe**: `#e6dad4` (Dividers, borders, technical marks)

## 3. Typography
- **Display & Headings**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights 700, 800, 900) — tight tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`), uppercase impact.
- **Body & Subtitles**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights 400, 500, 600) — relaxed leading (`1.6`), high legibility.
- **Metadata & Kicker**: `JetBrains Mono` (weights 500, 600) — all caps, loose tracking (`0.15em` to `0.25em`), tabular numbers.

## 4. Key Visual Asset
- **Asset**: `CTA.jpg`
- **Subject**: Hands holding 5 signature cups of Sekata drinks (Americano, Matcha, Lemon Tea, Cascara, Cold Brew) in front of a warm sunlit yellow knit sweater.
- **Emotional Message**: Abundance, community, ritual of gathering, welcoming friends to the slowbar table.
